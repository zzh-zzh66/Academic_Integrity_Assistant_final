咨询类循环检索子图：
user_query, refined_query, refined_keywords, query_complexity
    ↓
consult_retrieval_wrapper_node: 
    - 使用query_complexity调整检索策略
    - 输出 retrieval_results (原始检索结果)
    ↓
consult_context_expand_wrapper_node:
    - 输出 expanded_results (扩展后的结果)
    ↓
rerank_node (加权评分排序):  ← 这就是你问的节点！
    - 输入: expanded_results
    - 输出: ranked_results, weighted_score, top_score, top_3_avg
    ↓
context_extract_node:
    - 输入: top_3 ranked_results
    - 输出: structured_context (key_concepts, relation_map, missing_aspects, summary)
    ↓
improvement_analysis_node:
    - 输入: ranked_results, structured_context, history
    - 输出: improvement_potential, recommendation
    ↓
循环条件判断:
    - 综合分数、轮次、大模型建议
    - 决定: continue / exit_success / exit_fallback
